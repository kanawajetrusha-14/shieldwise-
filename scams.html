Know the Scam. Stop the Scam.

Scammers use different techniques to trick people into sharing money, passwords or personal information. Learn the common warning signs before you respond.

💳
High Risk

UPI Payment Scam

Scammers may pretend to send or receive money and trick users into approving a payment request.

🔐
High Risk

OTP Scam

A scammer may pretend to be from a bank or company and ask you to reveal an OTP.

🎣
High Risk

Phishing

Fake emails, messages or websites are used to steal login credentials or personal information.

💼
Medium Risk

Job Scam

Fake recruiters may promise attractive jobs and ask for registration or processing fees.

🏦
High Risk

KYC Scam

Fraudsters may claim that your KYC has expired and ask you to click a link or share information.

🎁
High Risk

Lottery & Prize Scam

You receive a message claiming that you have won money or a prize that you never entered for.

📱
High Risk

QR Code Scam

A QR code may redirect you to a payment request or a fake website designed to steal information.

📲
Medium Risk

Social Media Scam

Fake profiles, hacked accounts and fraudulent offers can be used to trick social media users.

🔍

No scam found

🛡️ Golden Rules of Online Safety

01 Never share OTP

Keep OTPs and passwords private.

02 Verify before paying

Check payment requests carefully.

03 Don't trust urgency

Take time to verify unexpected requests.

04 Check website addresses

Look carefully before entering information.
